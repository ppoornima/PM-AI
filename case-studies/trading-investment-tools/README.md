# Trading and Investment Tools

**Independent product development · Retail investing · Options research · Portfolio analysis**

A collection of personal tools for researching stock and options opportunities and reviewing existing investments.

## Tools covered

- **Portfolio Intelligence:** investment theses, holdings, catalysts, and decision history alongside market context.
- **Wheels Screener:** candidate comparison and scoring explanations for cash-secured puts and covered calls.
- **LEAPS Scanner:** staged screening and deeper analysis of long-dated options candidates.
- **Swing Trading Tools:** watchlist screening, ticker analysis, and options-contract review.
- **Multi-Timeframe and Options Analytics:** consolidated price, volatility, and options-positioning analysis.
